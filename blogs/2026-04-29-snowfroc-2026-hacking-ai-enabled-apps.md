This is a talk that IronCore CEO Patrick Walsh delivered first at the SnowFROC conference. It contains exploit demos, data compromises, and hardening patterns for AI-integrated applications. There's a focus on recent agentic systems like OpenClaw and some suprprisingly optimistic results as Patrick shows just how far AI security has come in the last few months (and how far it still has to go).
